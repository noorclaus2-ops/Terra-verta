About

A company building AI-powered bioproduction for industrial biofertilizers.

Our mission

Fertilizers feed the crops that feed the world. We believe a growing share of them can be made by biology.

Terra Verta exists to make biofertilizers an industrial reality: produced at scale, with consistent quality.

What is a biofertilizer?

A biofertilizer contains living microorganisms, such as bacteria or fungi. Applied to seed, plant or soil, they help the crop take up nutrients, for example by fixing nitrogen from the air or by releasing phosphorus that is locked in the soil.

How we work

  1. 1

    AI-guided design

    Our AI models help select the microorganisms and growing conditions with the best chance of success, so we get there with fewer trials.

  2. 2

    Precision fermentation

    We grow the microorganisms in controlled bioreactors, where every batch is measured and steered.

  3. 3

    Industrial production

    We scale the process up to industrial volumes and formulate the result into a product that is ready to use.
